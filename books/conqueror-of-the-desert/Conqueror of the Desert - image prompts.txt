THE CONQUEROR OF THE DESERT — IMAGE PROMPTS
25 pictures: the cover + one for each of the 24 chapters.
(When a chapter runs over two pages, its picture appears on both.)

START EVERY PROMPT WITH THIS STYLE LINE
--------------------------------------
Soft watercolour children's picture-book illustration with hand-drawn ink lines, warm natural light, Middle Eastern setting, present day, portrait orientation 3:4.

END EVERY PROMPT WITH
---------------------
No text, no words, no letters, no page numbers.

CHARACTERS (paste the ones on the page; attach an earlier picture as a reference once you have one)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- YASMINE: girl about 9, olive skin, big curious brown eyes, dark wavy hair in a ponytail with a yellow ribbon, green T-shirt, light trousers, sandals. Carries a small blue watering jug.
- HER BROTHER: boy about 6, short dark hair, orange T-shirt, shorts, a football.
- BABA (her father): man in his 40s, short beard, kind eyes, glasses, plain shirt with rolled sleeves.
- ABU NUMAN: very old man, white beard, deeply wrinkled kind face, white keffiyeh, long grey-brown robe, wooden walking stick. Leads a brown-and-white goat on a rope.
- THE OLD OLIVE TREE: a single old olive tree with a twisted silver trunk next to a small white desert house; at first its branches are dry with very few leaves.
- THE GARDEN: a small patch beneath the olive tree with low desert plants and bright yellow wildflowers.
- ANIMALS: two grey pigeons; a slender sand-coloured gazelle; three brown hens and a red rooster.

PROMPTS
-------
COVER: Yasmine kneeling beneath the old olive tree beside a small house in the golden desert, watering a patch of bright yellow flowers with her blue jug; behind her, sand dunes stretch to the horizon, while in the distance a small green orchard rises out of the sand. Very old Abu Numan with his walking stick and goat watches kindly from the side. Warm sunset light, space at the top.

1. YASMINE'S GREEN WORLD: Yasmine running happily through tall grass and wildflowers on a green mountainside, oak forest behind her, a sparkling river below, butterflies around her; in a corner, she drops flower seeds into a small cloth bag.

2. A DIFFERENT HOME: Yasmine standing in front of a small white house surrounded by endless sand dunes, holding her little cloth bag and looking around, a little sad; next to the house stands a lonely old olive tree with dry, tired branches.

3. NOTHING: Yasmine crouching in the shade of the old olive tree, staring at a bare patch of watered sand, chin in her hand, disappointed; her blue jug beside her. Hot, quiet afternoon.

4. SOMETHING IS ALIVE!: Yasmine frozen in surprise beneath the olive tree, a football rolling away behind her, staring at tiny green shoots pushing up through the sand; some already show bright yellow flowers. Her little brother runs up in the background.

5. YASMINE'S LITTLE GARDEN: The patch beneath the olive tree now full of yellow desert flowers and low green plants; butterflies and small birds visit; Yasmine kneels among them with her blue jug, smiling.

6. A NEW VOICE: Early morning on the balcony of the small white house: Yasmine in pyjamas pulling her sleepy father by the hand, both looking toward the olive tree, where two grey pigeons carry tiny twigs to build a nest.

7. FOUR MONTHS: Evening: Yasmine and her father walking hand in hand past her little garden and climbing a high sand dune; the father looks thoughtful, Yasmine looks up at him, hopeful.

8. A SKY FULL OF STARS: Yasmine and her father lying on their backs on top of a sand dune at night, arms stretched out, gazing up at a breathtaking sky full of thousands of stars and the Milky Way; Yasmine's face full of wonder.

9. THE NIGHT VISITOR: Moonlit desert night: a graceful gazelle leaping between the dunes toward the little garden under the olive tree; Yasmine running down the dune after it, laughing, her father sitting up on the dune top, smiling.

10. FOUR NEW FRIENDS: Yasmine scattering grain beneath the olive tree for three brown hens and a red rooster beside a small wooden coop she has built; the hens scratch the soil, which looks loose and soft where they have dug.

11. GOODBYE: Yasmine wagging a finger at a red rooster that stares back at her, unimpressed, while she fills containers with grain and water in front of the coop; a packed family car waits in the background.

12. THE TREE AWAKENS: Yasmine hugging her happy hens and rooster, then looking up in amazement at a branch of the old olive tree covered in fresh green leaves; warm afternoon light.

13. THE STRANGER: Very old Abu Numan with his white keffiyeh and walking stick arriving across the sandy land toward the little garden, a brown-and-white goat pulling impatiently on its rope; Yasmine peeking shyly from behind the olive tree trunk.

14. THIEVES!: Yasmine, cross, pouring water from her blue jug over a long line of tiny ants carrying grain across the ground; behind her, Abu Numan raises a hand gently to stop her.

15. LOOK MORE CLOSELY: Yasmine and Abu Numan crouching together, following the line of ants to a hen pecking excitedly at the soil; in the distance, a small green orchard island stands in the middle of the sand.

16. THE VILLAGE THAT DISAPPEARED: Dreamlike scene drawn like a memory, softer and faded colours: an old village with stone houses surrounded by green gardens and orchards, slowly being covered by drifting sand as families walk away with their belongings toward a distant city.

17. THE LAST NEIGHBOUR: In faded memory colours: old Abu Numan (slightly younger, grey beard) sitting beneath a dried, leafless olive tree in a deserted orchard, two sheep beside him, tossing bread crumbs to a line of ants with a tender smile.

18. THE ANT MAN: In faded memory colours: Abu Numan digging small holes in the dry orchard soil and placing food scraps inside; ants swarming around; two villagers in the background shaking their heads and laughing at him.

19. THE RAIN: In faded memory colours: heavy rain falling over the abandoned orchard; a cut-away view of the ground shows water trickling down through ant tunnels deep into the soil, where tree roots are waiting.

20. THE ORCHARD AWAKENS: In faded memory colours: Abu Numan kneeling in his orchard, which has turned green, with fresh shoots on the old trees and small plants covering the ground; ants around his feet; his grown-up children standing behind him, amazed.

21. ABU NUMAN: Back in the present, full colours: Yasmine jumping to her feet beneath the olive tree, pointing at the old man with wide, astonished eyes; Abu Numan laughing gently, letting soil trickle through his fingers; his goat beside him.

22. YASMINE'S IDEA: Yasmine kneeling to dig a small hole and drop food scraps into it, ants already arriving; Abu Numan beside her, showing her how; hens and the goat nearby; the olive tree with pigeons above.

23. LITTLE BY LITTLE: A wide view of the desert land over the years: patches of green spreading between the dunes around several houses, families planting and watering, grass, bushes and young trees appearing; birds in the sky.

24. THE CONQUEROR OF THE DESERT: Years later: a grown-up Yasmine (about 20, same ponytail with a yellow ribbon) telling a story to a circle of children sitting beneath tall green trees in a village now surrounded by orchards; very old Abu Numan sits beside her, laughing; the desert visible far in the distance.
