LAND OF THE LITTLE SUNS — SAMIR'S JOURNEY — IMAGE PROMPTS
16 new pictures: pages 16 to 31.

START EVERY PROMPT WITH THIS STYLE LINE
--------------------------------------
Soft watercolour children's picture-book illustration with hand-drawn ink lines, warm golden light, olden times, portrait orientation 3:4.

END EVERY PROMPT WITH
---------------------
No text, no words, no letters, no page numbers.

ATTACH A REFERENCE: a picture of Samir from the book (for example page 6), so he keeps the same face.

CHARACTERS
----------
- SAMIR (traveller): thin boy about 11, short messy dark hair, sun-tanned olive skin, big dark eyes, faded beige thobe with a rope belt, a striped brown cloak over his shoulders, sandals for the journey, a leather saddlebag across his body; rides a brown horse.
- THE CARAVAN MERCHANTS: traders in turbans and colourful robes with camels and mules loaded with bales of silk and spices.
- THE ANATOLIAN FARMER: kind old man with a grey beard, a felt cap and a woollen vest, rough working hands.
- THE PORTUGUESE ORCHARD KEEPER: friendly middle-aged man with a short dark beard, a wide straw hat, a linen shirt with rolled sleeves and a leather apron.

PROMPTS
-------
PAGE 16 — رحلة سمير: Dawn at the edge of the mud-brick village among date palms on the Euphrates: Samir, in his striped cloak with a leather saddlebag, hugs his two little siblings (a girl about 7 in a headscarf, a boy about 5) goodbye, while his bearded uncle in a white keffiyeh holds the reins of a brown horse. Bittersweet, hopeful mood.

PAGE 17 — في بلاد الشام: Samir riding his brown horse beside a long caravan of camels loaded with bales of silk, walking along the Euphrates under a wide sky; he leans toward a smiling merchant in a turban who points proudly at a roll of shimmering silk.

PAGE 18: A busy covered market (souk) in old Damascus with stone arches and hanging lanterns; stalls overflowing with grapes, figs, apricots and pomegranates in bright colours; Samir, wide-eyed, holds a big red pomegranate while a cheerful fruit seller laughs and shrugs.

PAGE 19: Samir sitting alone under a shady tree at the edge of the market, eating seeds from a split-open pomegranate, thoughtful, the busy souk softly blurred behind him; late afternoon light.

PAGE 20 — في بلاد الأناضول: Green Anatolian mountains and valleys; a small stone farmhouse surrounded by fruit trees; an old farmer with a grey beard and felt cap welcomes Samir at the door with a plate of fruit; Samir's horse grazes nearby.

PAGE 21: In a sunny orchard, the old farmer shows Samir a tiny seed between his fingers while dropping other seeds into a small cloth pouch; branches around them heavy with fruit, some ripe, some still green; Samir watches closely, fascinated.

PAGE 22: Morning: Samir, about to leave, stands beside his horse in front of the farmhouse; the old farmer points far away toward the south-west, where the sky glows warm and sunny beyond the mountains.

PAGE 23 — الطريق إلى الغرب: A sequence on one page: Samir loading sacks onto a mule for a caravan, watering horses at a well, and picking fruit in someone's orchard — busy, tired but cheerful; different landscapes blending into each other.

PAGE 24: Night in the desert: Samir sitting alone by a small caravan campfire, wrapped in his cloak, looking at a single date in his open palm; above the fire, a faint dream-image of his two little siblings and the palm trees of his village. Quiet, homesick, then a small smile.

PAGE 25: Next day at a caravan stop: a travelling merchant with a big moustache describing something with round gestures of his hands, as if holding a small sun; Samir leaning forward eagerly, listening; camels resting behind them.

PAGE 26 — في بلاد البرتغال: Sunset over a small Portuguese village with white houses and red tile roofs; Samir on his tired horse stopping in a narrow lane, staring in wonder at orange trees with dark green leaves, glowing orange fruits shining like little suns among the branches.

PAGE 27: In the orange grove, the orchard keeper in a straw hat hands Samir a ripe orange; Samir cuts it in half with his knife, and the bright juicy inside glows; a burst of fresh spray and fragrance; Samir's delighted face.

PAGE 28: Samir and the orchard keeper talking seriously under the orange trees; the keeper explaining with open hands, Samir listening intently, half an orange in his hand; ripe and green oranges on the branches around them.

PAGE 29: Samir sitting on a low stone wall in the orchard, telling his story with gestures while the orchard keeper listens with a kind smile, one hand on Samir's shoulder.

PAGE 30: The orchard keeper teaching Samir how to plant: choosing healthy seeds, pressing them into moist soil in small clay pots, shading young sprouts; then filling Samir's saddlebag with oranges and handing him a few small seedlings in pots of earth.

PAGE 31 — العودة: After a sandstorm behind desert rocks, travellers shaking sand from their clothes; Samir kneeling, carefully re-planting a small uprooted seedling into a new clay pot with his bare hands; a merchant in a turban watches him, puzzled and amused.
