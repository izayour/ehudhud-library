LAND OF THE LITTLE SUNS (أرض الشموس الصغيرة) — IMAGE PROMPTS

Soft watercolour children's picture-book illustration with hand-drawn ink lines, warm golden light, an old Arab village of mud-brick houses among date palms on the bank of the Euphrates river, olden times, portrait orientation 3:4.

CHARACTERS (paste the ones on the page; attach an earlier picture as a reference once you have one)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- SAMIR: boy about 11, sun-tanned olive skin, short dark hair, thin, big dark eyes, a simple faded beige thobe (long tunic) with a rope belt, barefoot. Carries a few dates in his hand. Later, as a traveller: same thobe plus a striped cloak, a leather saddlebag, riding a brown horse.
- HIS SIBLINGS: two younger children (a girl about 7 with a headscarf, a boy about 5), thin, in simple clothes.
- THE MERCHANT: a foreign trader with very pale skin that has never seen the sun, a thin pointed moustache, a tall dark hat and a fine embroidered coat; sly, too-sweet smile. Clever and sneaky, not frightening.
- THE SHOP: a small mud-brick shop with a wooden counter piled with baskets of shiny, bright red strawberries that glow like jewels.
- SAMIR'S UNCLE: strong man in his 40s, black beard, white keffiyeh with black cord, dark robe.
- THE WISE SHEIKH: very old man, long white beard, white keffiyeh, brown cloak, wooden staff.
- THE VILLAGERS: men and women in simple Bedouin clothes, children in tunics.

PROMPTS
-------
COVER: Samir standing proudly between young orange trees heavy with glowing orange fruit like little suns, holding one orange up to the sky; date palms, the Euphrates and the village behind him; warm sunset light, space at the top.

1. Banu Saad's village on the bank of the Euphrates: date palms heavy with dates, golden wheat fields, villagers harvesting and children playing by the river. Peaceful and prosperous.

2. A pale-skinned foreign stranger in a tall hat and embroidered coat crossing the Euphrates on a small boat, arriving at the village; villagers greeting him warmly with dates and milk; a man trading dates for wheat in the background.

3. The merchant's new little shop, its counter piled with baskets of shining red strawberries; a crowd of amazed villagers and children staring at them, eyes wide, as if looking at jewels.

4. Village boys boasting to each other in an alley, one pretending to describe the taste with big gestures; another villager hiding a single strawberry behind his back; the shop in the distance.

5. Samir walking past the shop at dusk holding a few dates in his hand, gazing longingly at the strawberries; inside the shop, the merchant leans forward and beckons him with a curled finger.

6. Samir at the shop counter, nervous and fascinated, staring at a single red strawberry the merchant holds out to him with a sly smile.

7. Samir standing in front of the counter, looking down at the dates in his open hand, torn between the strawberry and his hungry siblings; a faint thought-image of his two little siblings above him.

8. The merchant leaning over the counter, smiling sweetly, offering a small strawberry in exchange for half of Samir's dates; Samir hesitating, two loaves of bread pictured in a thought bubble.

9. Samir with a strawberry in his mouth, his face turning from joy to disappointment, lips puckered from the sour taste; the merchant watching from behind the counter.

10. Samir arguing angrily at the counter while the merchant shrugs innocently and dangles a big strawberry in front of him.

11. Samir biting into the big strawberry and finding that inside it is white and pale under a thin red skin; his face full of shock and regret; his empty hand with no dates left.

12. Samir alone at night, empty-handed, walking away from the village into the dark wilderness, looking back sadly at the lights of his home.

13. Night: villagers on horses with flaming torches searching the palm groves; Samir's uncle reaching up to Samir, who clings, exhausted, to the trunk of a tall date palm, while wolves with glowing eyes circle below. Tense but safe, not scary.

14. A gathering of village elders sitting on rugs in a mud-brick room lit by oil lamps; one man standing and shouting angrily, the wise old sheikh raising his hand calmly; Samir sitting quietly in a corner, wrapped in a blanket.

15. Samir standing up in the middle of the elders' gathering, determined, his hand on his chest, making his promise; everyone turning to look at him.

16. Samir riding away on a brown horse at dawn with a saddlebag; then, in the same picture or as a second scene, villagers gathered at the village entrance watching a lone rider return from the far horizon.

17. Samir standing on the flat roof of a mud-brick house, arms open, speaking to a large crowd of excited villagers gathered below.

18. Samir on the roof holding up a round orange that glows like a little sun, then cutting it in half with a knife to show its bright juicy inside; the crowd below gasping with joy.

19. Samir handing out slices of orange and single seeds to villagers, one per household; children holding seeds carefully in their palms.

20. Villagers kneeling beside tiny green orange sprouts in front of every house, watering them proudly like new parents; children watching closely.

21. Young orange trees with dark green leaves and white blossoms; women collecting the white flowers in baskets and making orange-blossom water in copper pots; small green fruits appearing on the branches.

22. Years later: the village of Banu Saad transformed into a green oasis of orange groves full of glowing oranges like little suns among the palms, visitors arriving to admire them; in the corner, the merchant leaving on a boat across the Euphrates, his shop closed behind him.
