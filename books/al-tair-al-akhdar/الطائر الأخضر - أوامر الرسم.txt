THE GREEN BIRD — IMAGE PROMPTS
20 pictures: the cover + 19 story pages.

START EVERY PROMPT WITH THIS STYLE LINE
--------------------------------------
Soft watercolour children's picture-book illustration, hand-drawn ink lines, warm gentle light, Levantine village and forest in olden times, portrait orientation (3:4).

CHARACTERS (paste the ones that appear on the page, and attach an earlier picture as a reference once you have one)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- ALI: boy about 9, short dark curly hair, olive skin, big brown eyes, plain cream tunic with a brown sash, barefoot.
- HANIYA: girl about 11, long dark braid, olive skin, kind brown eyes, faded rose-red dress with a small embroidered collar.
- THE FATHER: gentle man in his 40s, short dark beard, white keffiyeh, dark brown robe.
- THE STEPMOTHER: woman in her 30s, sharp eyebrows, dark-green dress, gold bangles, a sly look. Never frightening; more silly and sneaky.
- THE GREEN BIRD: small bird, size of a sparrow, glossy bright emerald-green feathers, bright brown eyes.
- THE GAZELLE: slender young gazelle, golden-brown coat with a faint green shimmer, striking green eyes.
- THE PRINCE: young man about 20, kind face, short beard, embroidered blue vest, white shirt, sash, bow on his back; rides a white horse.
- THE WELL: old round stone well at the edge of a forest, ivy on the stones. Inside, halfway down the wall, ONE stone sticks out and glows faintly green.

PROMPTS
-------
COVER: The small emerald-green bird flying in front of the old ivy-covered stone well at the edge of a magical forest; Ali and Haniya holding hands nearby, looking up at it in wonder. Golden late-afternoon light, room at the top for the title.

PAGE 1: Ali and Haniya laughing as they run through golden wheat fields toward a small stone village; their father watching from a doorway with a smile; forest on the hills behind.

PAGE 2: The stepmother alone in an old village kitchen beside a big cooking pot, cheeks full, a spoon in her hand, guiltily looking at the nearly empty pot; steam rising, copper pans on the wall. Comic, not scary.

PAGE 3: The stepmother leading Ali by the hand along a forest path toward the old stone well at the forest's edge; Ali looks unsure, she looks sly; long evening shadows.

PAGE 4: View from inside the well: Ali sitting alone on the mossy bottom, a small puddle of water and a few mushrooms beside him, looking up at the round patch of sky far above; one stone halfway up glows faintly green.

PAGE 5: Inside the well: Ali standing on the glowing stone, half-transformed, his arms turning into green wings and emerald feathers appearing on his body, an amazed look on his face; sparkles of green light.

PAGE 6: Haniya at a wooden window in the evening, chin in her hands, looking out at the road; a small emerald-green bird perched on a branch just outside, looking straight at her.

PAGE 7: Haniya asleep in her bed at night, moonlight through the window; above her, in a soft dream cloud, the green bird singing to her.

PAGE 8: Haniya and Ali hugging joyfully at the bottom of the well, a few green feathers floating around them; a ray of sunlight falling from the top.

PAGE 9: Haniya climbing down into the well on the old rope while Ali, below, raises his hands in warning; worried faces.

PAGE 10: The green bird flying out of the well over a huge magical forest with tall twisted trees and glowing flowers; Haniya waving from the well below.

PAGE 11: The green bird perched on a rock above a small clear spring in the forest; in the shimmering surface of the water, the faint reflection of a cat; a second spring further along the path shows the reflection of a dog.

PAGE 12: Ali, half-way between boy and gazelle, drinking from a third spring between old trees; the gazelle form appearing in green sparkles, with striking green eyes.

PAGE 13: The gazelle trapped in a rope hunting net in the forest, struggling, with a sad and worried expression; a basket of spilled fruits and roots beside it.

PAGE 14: The kind young prince kneeling beside the netted gazelle, his bow lowered, gently cutting the net with a small knife; a tear in the gazelle's green eye; his white horse and two companions behind him.

PAGE 15: The gazelle running ahead and looking back over its shoulder; the prince following on his white horse through the forest toward the old well.

PAGE 16: The prince leaning over the edge of the well, astonished, looking down at Ali and Haniya, who look up at him from the bottom; the gazelle's green sparkles fading around Ali.

PAGE 17: Workers with picks and lanterns at the end of a tunnel dug into the side of a hill; Ali and Haniya stepping out of the tunnel into bright sunlight, the prince welcoming them.

PAGE 18: Ali and Haniya running into their father's open arms in front of their village home; the father crying with joy; the stepmother leaving down the road with her bundle, looking ashamed; the prince on his horse nearby.

PAGE 19: The old ivy-covered well in the middle of the forest on a quiet moonlit night; a small emerald-green bird circling above it among the branches. Peaceful and magical.
